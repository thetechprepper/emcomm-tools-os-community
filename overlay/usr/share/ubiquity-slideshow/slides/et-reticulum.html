Reticulum

et-reticulum provides plug-and-play support for running the Reticulum Network Stack (RNS) with zero configuration. It includes sample "blueprint" configurations for quickly starting a Reticulum node that can communicate over LoRa, packet radio, modem73, the Mercury HF modem, Wi-Fi, Ethernet, and even the Internet—if you must.

The Heltec v4 is the officially supported LoRa device for EmComm Tools.
